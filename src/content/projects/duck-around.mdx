Made in two days for Shift Game Jam 2026 with a team of four, on the theme "Festival of Passports". It won first place.

The whole game hangs on one gimmick: you hold up a magnifying lens and see straight through the suitcases on the belt.
That effect, and most of what's on screen, was my part.

## The lens

It's a stencil trick, so it costs almost nothing:

- The lens renders first (`Geometry-1`) with `ColorMask 0`, so it's invisible, and writes `2` into the stencil buffer wherever it covers the screen.
- The suitcase exteriors only draw where the stencil is **not** `2`, so they vanish inside the lens.
- The interiors render after them (`Geometry+1`) and only draw where the stencil **is** `2`, so you only ever see the contents through the glass.

A teammate later built the raised-lens and reveal variants on top of it.

## The look

- **Outline.** Sobel edge detection in a Shader Graph custom function node, run as a full-screen pass. A convolution is far cleaner as a few lines of HLSL than as a node graph.
- **Posterisation** to flatten the shading into bands that sit well with the outlines.
- **Procedural sky.** A gradient, a star-generation sub-graph and HLSL cartoon clouds built from ellipse SDFs scattered on a hashed grid.
- **Decals** on the bags and floor.
- I modelled the tables and the roof.

## Procedural suitcases

Bags spawn along the conveyor with randomised orientation, colour, contents and decals, so every belt load looks different and you actually have to look.
