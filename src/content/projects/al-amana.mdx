A 3D adventure made in three days for the Heritage Gameathon. You cross the desert at night to keep a promise, and the
whole thing lives or dies on mood, so the visuals were the job I took.

## The look

The target was a limited palette with ordered dithering, flat colour regions and a hard screen-space outline, more like a
printed illustration than a lit 3D scene.

- **Post stack.** I wrote the dither, colour-map and screen-outline passes myself, using NullTale's
  [VolFx](https://x.com/NullTale) as a reference for the approach. The work was in making them read together: the order of
  the passes, the palette ramps, and tinting the scene before the dither so the colours survive quantisation instead of
  collapsing into noise.
- **Sand dunes.** A dune shader that keeps the ridges readable after posterisation.
- **Day–night transition** and a **view-angle effect** as Shader Graph shaders.

## Lighting and particles

I lit the scenes for the post stack rather than for realism: big shapes of light and dark that dither cleanly. Particle
systems follow set movement paths, so sand and embers drift where the level wants your eye to go.
