HexaSentinal is an offline platform for digital forensics: getting evidence in securely, analysing it and reporting on it.

As technical lead I architected it as a modular, local-first system:

- **Evidence integrity.** Evidence is stored immutably, hashed cryptographically on ingestion, and every action is audit-logged.
- **Processing pipeline.** OCR, speech-to-text, indexing and AI-assisted analysis run as stages, feeding into integrated reporting.
